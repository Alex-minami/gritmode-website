// KNOWLEDGE_BASE

Honest answers.
Nothing hidden.

Apple Vision Framework tracks key body points (shoulders, hips, ankles). A rep is only counted when a full movement cycle completes with a minimum interval and amplitude check. Waving your arms or rocking the phone won't work.
No. All recognition happens on-device. Not a single frame leaves your iPhone — not to a server, not to analytics. Real-time processing via Vision + Core ML.
When your balance runs out but you need an app right now — you borrow time. The next earned minutes automatically repay the debt first. You can accumulate up to 500 minutes of debt.
University College London study (Lally et al., 2009): a new habit forms in an average of 66 days — not 21, as often claimed. We give you exactly that window, with a small daily barrier instead of an outright ban.
For real blocking — yes. This is Apple's standard API (Family Controls). Without it, GritMode can count exercise but can't physically block selected apps.
iOS 17.6+ only for now. We use Apple Vision and Screen Time API — Android has no real equivalents. Considering a port, but no timeline yet.
